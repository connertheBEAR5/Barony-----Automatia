Barony Automatia - Illusion Magic School Design and Developer Guide
==================================================================

Status
------

Illusion Magic is one part of the optional, server-authoritative Automatian
Mode gameplay group. Automatian Mode is OFF in the normal default `svFlags`
value and jointly enables the Illusion school and its spellbooks, Skill
Books/Skill Scrolls, and the Magic Grimoire. Start a host, dedicated server,
or local test with:

    ./build-super-posix/barony --automatian-mode

The host's synchronized `SV_FLAG_AUTOMATIAN_MODE` bit is authoritative. A
client cannot turn the mode on locally for an off session. The old
`--illusion-magic` and `--skill-books` command-line options remain
compatibility aliases for `--automatian-mode`; none of them enables only a
single component. Treat the mode as a startup/session option; changing it in
the middle of a live game is not a supported content migration.

When the flag is off, the Illusion filter is absent, Illusion spell and
spellbook slots are disabled/hidden, books do not enter ordinary random item
generation, books refuse to teach or consume, `addSpell()` refuses new
Illusion learning, and every Illusion cast/runtime hook returns without an
effect. Existing saves may retain a previously learned append-only spell ID;
it remains hidden and inert until an enabled session loads it.


Registry and compatibility architecture
---------------------------------------

Existing spell IDs 0 through 224 are unchanged. The new IDs are append-only:

| ID | Spell | MP | Required rank | Target | Fixed duration |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 225 | Mirror Other | 8 | 0 Novice | nearby actor | 500 ticks / 10 s |
| 226 | Mirror Copy | 14 | 40 Skilled | nearby enemy | 300 ticks / 6 s |
| 227 | Mirror Wall | 8 | 20 Basic | floor tile | 400 ticks / 8 s |
| 228 | Mirror Reflect | 24 | 60 Expert | self | 300 ticks / 6 s |
| 229 | Mirror Mimic | 20 | 60 Expert | nearby enemy | 500 ticks / 10 s |
| 230 | Mirror Reflect Loot | 18 | 60 Expert | owned inventory item | 300 ticks / 6 s visual |
| 231 | Phantasm Path | 20 | 60 Expert | floor/gap endpoint | 400 ticks / 8 s |
| 232 | Mirror Duplicate Loot | 100 | 100 Legendary | owned inventory item | instant after selection |
| 233 | Mirage Wall | 10 | 20 Basic | real wall tile | 400 ticks / 8 s |
| 234 | Paranoia | 25 | 60 Expert | nearby hostile area | 250 ticks / 5 s |
| 235 | Vertical Mirage | 12 | 40 Skilled | real floor tile | 300 ticks / 6 s |
| 236 | Shadow Step | 12 | 40 Skilled | self/exact backward tile | 100-tick / 2 s decoy |
| 237 | Store Magic | 12 + 1 sustain | 80 Master | self | sustained |

There is one real `SPELLBOOK_*` item for every row, appended after every
pre-existing item and Magic Grimoire. The books use the existing spellbook
inventory, equip, reading, learning, hotbar, tooltip, editor lookup, and random
generation paths. Their definitions and names are installed after base JSON is
loaded so the shipped legacy JSON remains readable. They borrow established
Barony spellbook/icon assets; no parallel item registry exists.

Illusion is a first-class derived school rather than a fourth serialized
`PROFICIENCIES[]` member. Adding a fourth fixed member would change existing
character save and packet layouts. Effective Illusion proficiency is therefore:

    max(modified Sorcery, modified Mysticism, modified Thaumaturgy), clamped 0..100

All learning checks, spellbook failure/extra-mana chances, casting animation,
tooltips, and Store Magic progression call the same resolver. Each registry
spell retains Mysticism as its internal compatibility skill so old generic
spell structures remain valid, while UI labels and decisions use Illusion.


Spell behavior
--------------

Mirror Other
    Copies a selected actor's rendered appearance onto a hologram proxy that
    follows the caster. The caster remains the real target/collision/identity
    entity. No name authority, faction, party, owner, inventory, stats, quest,
    dialogue, persistence, or player identity is copied. Mirror Mimic is
    cleared when this effect begins.

Mirror Copy
    Creates a temporary HOLOGRAM shadow at the target. It is harmless,
    non-recruitable, cannot own inventory/state, and produces no real loot or
    XP. The normal authoritative entity path replicates the visual, while the
    bounded Illusion runtime retires it at expiry.

Mirror Wall
    Requires open supported ground. It creates a passable wall visual plus a
    `FakeWall` navigation overlay. Eligible hostiles route around the perceived
    wall. The real floor/obstacle map bytes and collision are never changed.

Mirror Reflect
    Applies its own named ward, distinct from normal Reflection. At projectile
    impact it may reflect eligible hostile magic only if normal Reflection did
    not already resolve it. The reflected payload receives a modest 20 percent
    damage bonus (minimum one). Existing reflection counters plus the dedicated
    Mirror-reflected guard prevent loops. Reflection, Absorb, Seize, Store, and
    Mirror Reflect payloads are excluded.

Mirror Mimic
    Refuses bosses. It creates an appearance proxy like Mirror Other and adds a
    temporary perception query: eligible hostile AI may treat the caster as
    neutral. It never rewrites faction, allegiance, party, recruitment,
    persistent identity, inventory, or quest state. Overt behavior and normal
    engine checks can still reveal/attack the caster. Mirror Other is cleared
    when this effect begins.

Mirror Reflect Loot
    Opens the normal item-effect inventory selector for an item the authenticated
    caster currently owns. The result is only a bounded, non-interactable visual
    entity. It has no `Item`, quantity, stable ID, value, pickup, equipment,
    crafting, quest, shop, or persistence identity. The picker and result say
    `FAKE / ILLUSORY` explicitly.

Phantasm Path
    Builds at most four valid unsupported/liquid tiles between caster and the
    selected endpoint. The overlay supplies ground only to its owner and the
    bounded server-derived authorized follower/allied actor set. It is not a
    global solid bridge and unauthorized hostiles receive no support. Visuals
    do not mutate floor, lava, water, obstacle, or structural data.

Mirror Duplicate Loot
    Charges the full 100 MP once in the normal cast pipeline, then opens the
    owned-item selector for 20 seconds without a second charge. On successful
    server revalidation it creates exactly one real item preserving type,
    status, appearance, and identified state. Quantity is forced to one.
    Positive beatitude becomes equally cursed, zero becomes -1, and an already
    cursed item stays cursed; duplication never improves curse state.

    The safety resolver rejects spell pseudo-items; all keys; artifact equipment
    and orbs; Magic Grimoire; quest/progression-tagged items; loot bags,
    backpacks, and container-tagged items; and S.A.M. runtime items without a
    currently registered non-empty stable ID. The server finds a matching item
    in the authenticated player's live inventory and repeats every check. The
    successful copy enters the normal pickup/inventory path and therefore uses
    normal real-item persistence.

Mirage Wall
    Requires a real obstacle tile. A renderer overlay suppresses only that
    wall's visual faces. Real tile data, collision, pathing, line-of-world
    geometry, persistence, and structural layers remain unchanged.

Paranoia
    Applies temporary confusion-style target perception to eligible hostile
    non-boss monsters within 48 world units on the caster's exact playable
    floor. It does not permanently change factions or player ownership.

Vertical Mirage
    Requires a real supported floor. The renderer hides that floor as a false
    pit and eligible hostile path maps avoid the perceived drop. Players and
    physics retain the real floor support; map data is unchanged.

Shadow Step
    Computes exactly four tiles (64 world units) backward from current facing.
    The destination must be in bounds, have real floor on the caster's exact
    playable floor, and have no obstacle. The normal authoritative teleport
    helper must then succeed. It never shortens the distance or searches past a
    wall/void. Success leaves a harmless two-second hologram at the old point;
    failure retires the provisional clone.

Store Magic
    Applies the existing sustained-spell model and owns a finite server vault
    keyed to the runtime owner UID. Only a hostile projectile with an ordinary
    player-learnable spellbook mapping is eligible. Reflection (26), Amplify
    Magic (44), Overcharge (100), Absorb Magic (155), Seize Magic (156), Mirror
    Reflect (228), Mirror Duplicate Loot (232), and Store Magic (237) are
    explicitly excluded. Touch, self, area, trap, hidden NPC/debug, custom
    no-book, and bespoke Illusion casts are excluded rather than guessed safe.


Store Magic deterministic rules
-------------------------------

Capacity uses the real derived proficiency thresholds:

| Proficiency | Tier | Slots | Release choice | Study |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| 0-19 | Novice | 1 | deterministic random | no |
| 20-39 | Basic | 2 | deterministic random | no |
| 40-59 | Skilled | 3 | oldest or newest | no |
| 60-79 | Expert | 4 | exact slot; exact discard | no |
| 80-99 | Master | 5 | exact slot; exact discard | yes |
| 100 | Legendary | 5 | exact slot; exact discard | yes |

Random selection is deterministic from authoritative tick and owner UID. A
capture stores pointer-free values only: spell ID, original effective mana,
captured power, original caster proficiency, capture tick, study ticks, and
whether a normal book makes it player-learnable. A full vault refuses capture
and lets projectile resolution continue to later defenses/impact.

Release mana:

    base = max(1, ceil(original_effective_mana / 4))
    deficit = original_caster_proficiency - current_illusion_proficiency
    if deficit >= 20:
        base += ceil(deficit / 20)

Release power:

    delta = current_illusion_proficiency - original_caster_proficiency
    if delta < 0: percent = max(50, 100 + delta / 2)
    else:         percent = min(115, 100 + delta / 4)
    result = max(1, captured_power * percent / 100)
    if native_power > 1: result = min(result, native_power - 1)

The server validates the selected tier/action, spell definition, current
sustained state, vault contents, and MP before it removes the entry. The stored
spell is copied and released through the normal cast system under a guarded
release context, so Store does not recapture or re-charge its activation cost.

At proficiency 80+, each player-learnable entry accrues study only while Store
Magic is sustained. At 15,000 ticks (five minutes at 50 Hz), it is removed and
taught through the normal `addSpell` path, producing normal learning feedback
and known-spell persistence. The vault itself is deliberately runtime-only: it
is cleared on feature shutdown, MapInstance activation, owner disappearance,
and process/session restart.

Incoming projectile resolution is exclusive and ordered:

    spell immunity
      -> Store Magic
      -> ordinary Reflection
      -> Mirror Reflect
      -> Absorb Magic
      -> normal impact

One successful branch consumes the projectile outcome. The client never sends
a stored spell ID, power, owner UID, MapInstance, or playable floor mutation.


Playable-Z, MapInstance, AI, and rendering
------------------------------------------

Every navigation/visual overlay has one `SpatialScope` containing the current
authoritative `WorldInstanceIdentity::key()` and one explicit `playableFloor`.
Queries must match both. Same X/Y on another floor, `authoredMapLayer`, local
`Entity::z`, camera Z, and a coincident map name are never substitutes.

The registry permits at most 32 simultaneous overlays, eight tiles per overlay,
and 32 authorized actor UIDs per overlay. Phantasm Path currently creates at
most four tiles. Holograms are bounded to 32 and fake-loot visuals to 16; an old
visual is retired before a new one exceeds its bound. Runtime vault owners are
bounded to `MAXPLAYERS + 32` and each vault is independently tier-capped. Expiry removes overlays
and increments the renderer revision. A MapInstance activation clears all
runtime UID associations before reused UIDs can bind to unrelated entities.

`paths.cpp` applies FakeWall/FakePit perception to the requesting eligible
actor's temporary path map. Phantasm support is actor-aware in player falling,
pit, and swimming/ground checks. `opengl.cpp` consults HiddenWall/FakePit only
for the rendered floor and invalidates cached chunks when the overlay revision
changes. No illusion writes map tile bytes or becomes Z4/Z5 structural state.


Networking and headless authority
----------------------------------

Normal spell requests, entity replication, feature flags, and known-spell
packets remain in use. Four bounded Illusion messages supplement recipient
views without changing legacy packet layouts:

* `ILOV`: server-to-client overlay snapshot, filtered to the client's current
  active MapInstance; includes authoritative floor, expiry, bounded tiles, and
  bounded authorized actor UIDs.
* `ILIT`: client-to-server item-selection request. The packet's player byte is
  authenticated by the existing sender decoder; the server ignores claimed
  ownership and searches the authenticated inventory. S.A.M. requests append a
  stable string ID and are rejected if its runtime mapping does not match.
* `ILVA`: server-to-owning-client vault snapshot. Other players do not receive
  private vault contents.
* `ILVR`: client-to-server release/discard request. The authenticated server
  derives owner, proficiency, permitted selection mode, current vault, effect,
  MP, and spell definition.

Packet handlers enforce direction. Host/headless execution owns casts,
projectile defenses, AI perception, overlays, item creation, teleport, vault,
study, and learning. Late/reconnecting clients derive the feature bit from the
session and receive fresh relevant overlay/vault snapshots during normal ticks.


Persistence and S.A.M. 2.1
--------------------------

* Known Illusion spells: normal existing player known-spell save path.
* Illusion spellbooks: normal items; ordinary generation only while enabled.
* A legitimate real duplicate: normal item persistence.
* Holograms, disguise proxies, fake loot, wall/pit/path overlays: never saved.
* Stored Magic Vault: explicitly runtime-only and never serialized.

Existing item IDs stay stable because all books were appended. S.A.M. keeps its
reserved custom runtime-ID space and stable IDs remain persistence/network
authority. Fake loot never receives a stable ID. A real custom duplicate is
allowed only when the active S.A.M. registry proves a stable ID; the `ILIT`
request carries that ID so a stale/different runtime mapping cannot be used.


UI and authoring integration
----------------------------

With the flag on, the normal spell panel adds an Illusion filter after
Thaumaturgy, all known Illusion spells use normal hotbar behavior, each spell
and book receives a code-owned detailed tooltip, and effect entries distinguish
Mirror Other, Mirror Mimic, Mirror Reflect, and Store Magic. The Store status
icon displays `used/capacity` and opens an embedded vault view. The loot spells
reuse the established item-effect selector with unmistakable fake/real labels.

The code-owned definitions give the editor and generic spell/item lookups the
new names and fixed numeric IDs without editing legacy JSON. An explicit map or
debug tool can retain a numeric book reference even with the runtime flag off,
but ordinary gameplay cannot use the book until enabled.

For exact screen behavior, input, focus, low-resolution constraints, and manual
checks, see `helpful stuff/Illusion Magic School UI and UX Guide.txt`.


Developer extension rules
-------------------------

1. Append IDs; never insert into an old numeric range.
2. Add immutable metadata to `illusion_magic_model.*`, then route gameplay
   through the normal `castSpell` authority boundary.
3. Use `proficiencyForSpell()` / `getEffectiveSpellcastingAbility()` for every
   Illusion requirement, fumble, and display decision.
4. Scope any spatial state with active MapInstance plus explicit
   `playableFloor`. Never infer either from `Entity::z`.
5. Keep temporary perception in bounded overlays/effects; do not mutate tiles,
   permanent factions, identity, parties, or persistence.
6. Treat item appearance as presentation, never identity. Revalidate live
   server inventory and S.A.M. stable definitions before real creation.
7. Insert new projectile defenses into the one exclusive impact decision,
   with loop guards and deterministic tests.
8. Keep the off path inert in registry consumers, generation, learning, UI,
   casting, networking, AI, and tick logic.
9. Extend the comparison audit and deterministic model/source contracts before
   claiming a new behavior supported.


Validation and manual acceptance boundary
-----------------------------------------

`tests/illusion_magic_tests.cpp` deterministically covers registry values and
IDs, proficiency tiers, vault selection/capacity/full/discard/study behavior,
mana/power formulas, store and duplicate safety, every overlay kind,
MapInstance/floor isolation, bounds, and source contracts for all 13 spells,
books, flag, UI, packets, lifecycle, and defense order.

Automated source/model coverage is not a substitute for rendered gameplay.
Before release, run one headless host with `--automatian-mode` and two real
clients. Give both clients books, learn and hotbar all spells, observe every
effect on two playable floors, put the clients at equal X/Y on different
floors, then put them in divergent MapInstances. Confirm no overlay, disguise,
support, path avoidance, vault identity, or renderer state leaks. Reconnect one
client and confirm the authoritative flag plus relevant overlay/vault snapshot.
Confirm fake loot cannot be picked up and a permitted real duplicate survives
a normal save/reload. Repeat with a registered S.A.M. item and a rejected
unregistered/stale mapping. Exercise Store capture/release/full/learning and
the full defense order. Finally disable Automatian Mode and verify the normal
UI, generation, learning, and runtime are unchanged. Also confirm that the
Magic Grimoire and Skill Manual paths are unavailable in that off session.

This implementation does not begin Z5 or Z6.
