CUSTOM DIALOGUE QUEST SCOPE AND REPEATABILITY
================================================

Purpose
-------
Quest ownership and repeatability are separate ideas.

scope
    Decides who owns one quest-progress record.

repeatable
    Decides whether that owner may reset and complete it again.

JSON
----
Add these fields inside the root quest object:

{
    "quest": {
        "title": "A Light in the Dark",
        "scope": "player",
        "repeatable": false
    }
}

Scopes
------
player
    Every player receives independent progress.

party
    All members of one party share progress.

world
    The persistent world has one shared progress record.

Repeatability
-------------
false
    The owner may complete the quest once.

true
    The owner may reset and complete the quest again according to
    repeat/reset rules.

Your required behavior
----------------------
For a quest every player may complete once:

    "scope": "player",
    "repeatable": false

Player A may complete it.
Player C may later complete it independently.
Neither player may complete it a second time.

Current runtime support
-----------------------
This stage safely supports only:

    player + repeatable false

The parser recognizes party, world, and repeatable true, but rejects
those definitions with a clear log message until their authoritative
sharing and reset rules are implemented.

This avoids silently treating a party or repeatable quest as a normal
one-time player quest.

Future combinations
-------------------
player + repeatable true
    Independent reset per player.

party + repeatable false
    One completion shared by current party members.

party + repeatable true
    Party-owned repeat cycle.

world + repeatable false
    One permanent world completion.

world + repeatable true
    World-owned reset cycle.
