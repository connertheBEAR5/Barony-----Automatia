NATIVE QUEST BUTTON AND HUD TRACKING
=====================================

A new Quests button appears with Barony's existing inventory/status
navigation controls. It opens the same journal as the J key.

The journal now has a Track Quest / Untrack Quest button.

Only active quests may be tracked.

During normal gameplay, the tracked quest displays at the upper-right
of the player's virtual screen with:
- quest title,
- up to three unfinished visible objectives,
- optional objective labels.

The tracker hides while menus or the journal are open and after the
quest completes or fails.

Tracking is separate local UI state for each player and is session-only.
